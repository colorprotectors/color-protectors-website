FOR THE GROWN-UPS

A few little
things to know.

Is this a storybook or a coloring book?

Both! Short story captions follow Milo and Pip's adventure, alongside pictures to color and activities to explore.

What is inside the paperback?

The 70-page paperback includes 34 story, coloring, and activity pages with blank backs, plus a title page and a publishing page. You'll find story scenes, coloring illustrations, a maze, hidden crystals, and creative activities.

What coloring supplies work best?

Crayons and colored pencils are recommended. If using markers, place a protective sheet behind the page.

Can I buy the book here?

Not yet. Our website is open so you can meet the characters and preview the book. Orders will open at a later date, once payment and shipping are ready. We are not taking payments or preorders.

How will I pay when orders open?

We will use Stripe for secure online checkout when sales open. Checkout is currently unavailable, and we are not accepting payments or preorders. Available payment methods and the full order total will be shown before you pay.

How much is shipping?

The paperback is $9.99. Shipping is paid separately and is not included in that price. Shipping rates and delivery details will be confirmed before orders open. At checkout, you will see the shipping charge, any applicable tax, and your full order total before payment.
